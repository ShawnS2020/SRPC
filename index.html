Welcome to Our Church

Our church exists to glorify God and to teach His people to enjoy Him forever.

Southfield Reformed Presbyterian Church is a congregation of the Reformed Presbyterian Church of North America (RPCNA). We embrace the Scriptures of the Old and New Testaments as the inspired and inerrant Word of God, and we hold the Westminster Confession of Faith with the Larger and Shorter Catechisms as our standards.

Our History

In 1834, three years before Michigan gained its statehood, Scots-Irish settlers organized our congregation, the first church of any kind in Southfield. We still worship on Evergreen Road in the building raised in 1861, a small but energetic congregation continuing in the “old paths” of our godly forefathers.

The Authority of Scripture

The Bible, Old and New Testaments, is the inerrant, infallible Word of God and our only rule for faith and life. All of our beliefs stem from a full commitment to its authority.

The Triune God

There is one living and true God, eternally existing as three persons: the Father, the Son and the Holy Spirit, equal in power and glory.

Humanity and Sin

God created man in His own image. In Adam, humanity fell into sin, and every person is born guilty and unable to save himself.

Salvation in Christ Alone

Jesus Christ, the eternal Son of God, became man, lived in perfect obedience, died in the place of sinners and rose bodily from the dead. We are saved by grace alone, through faith alone, in Christ alone.

The Church and Presbyterian Government

Jesus Christ is the only King and Head of the church. Each congregation is overseen by elected elders, and elders from many congregations meet together in higher courts.

Christ’s Crown Rights

As Covenanters, we confess that Jesus Christ is King over every nation and every part of life, not only over the church.

Worship

The reading and exposition of the Word of God are the central focus of our worship. Our praise is the singing of the Psalms, without musical instruments.

The Sacraments

Baptism and the Lord’s Supper are the two sacraments Christ gave to His church. They are visible signs and seals of the gospel promises, received by faith.

Our Standards

We hold the Westminster Confession of Faith and the Larger and Shorter Catechisms as our subordinate standards, and the RPCNA Testimony as our continuing application of God’s Word to the world today.

Nearly Two Centuries of Faithful Witness

Founded in 1834 by Scottish Covenanter settlers, Southfield Reformed Presbyterian Church is one of the oldest congregations in metropolitan Detroit. For generations, we have held fast to the Reformed faith, worshipping God according to His Word and bearing witness to the Kingship of Jesus Christ over all of life.
