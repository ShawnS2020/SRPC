Finding Us

Southfield is a northern suburb of Detroit. Our building is on the east side of Evergreen Road, about a block and a half south of I-696, and is easy to reach by highway from every direction.

Accessibility

A wheelchair lift provides ground-level access to both the main auditorium and the church basement.
