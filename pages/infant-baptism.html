Lectures and sermons on the Scriptural basis for infant baptism and the status of covenant children.

See also: Covenant of Baptism

A four-part lecture series

Below is a four-part lecture series outlining the Scriptural basis for infant baptism, originally hosted on our congregation’s earlier website. The first lecture offers a survey of Scriptural teaching on the place of believers’ children in redemptive history, from Genesis to Revelation. The remaining lectures explore three simple arguments for infant baptism, based on a Scriptural understanding of the Covenant of Grace, the Church, and circumcision.

  1. Believers’ Children in Redemptive History

  2. The Covenant of Grace

  3. The Church

  4. Circumcision
