Our Leadership

Our Elders

  • Adam Kuehner Pastor
  • Jon Hughes Ruling Elder
  • John Kim Ruling Elder

Our congregation is governed by our pastor and two ruling elders. All three were elected by the members of our congregation and carry equal authority as overseers of Christ’s flock. A complete list of their ordination vows is available here.

Pastor Adam Kuehner was raised in a Christian home, where he trusted Jesus Christ as his personal Savior and Lord from an early age. After receiving his Master of Divinity degree from Reformed Presbyterian Theological Seminary, Adam was ordained as our pastor in 2012. His hobbies include reading, drinking coffee and tea, watching/playing baseball, and spending quality time with his family.
